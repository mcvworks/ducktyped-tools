WHOIS / IP Lookup

Look up a registered domain or IPv4 address. Leave blank to detect your public IP.

For current domain registration data, use ICANN’s RDAP lookup. This tool summarizes legacy WHOIS; missing fields do not prove availability. Results may be cached.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this WHOIS tool return?

For domains, the tool summarizes legacy WHOIS registration fields when available. Use ICANN Lookup for current RDAP information, especially when a result is empty or incomplete. IPv4 input returns network and approximate location information.

Does an empty result mean the domain is available?

No. Missing fields or a failed lookup do not prove that a domain can be registered. Confirm availability with the registry or a registrar.

Are results live and uncached?

Results may be cached. Verify renewal dates and transfer or hold statuses with the authoritative registration service and your registrar account before acting.

Can an IP lookup identify a person?

No. An ISP, organization, or approximate IP location does not establish a person’s identity or precise physical location.
