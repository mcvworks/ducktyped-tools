Device Lookup

Last updated: March 13, 2026

Identify a device from its IMEI number, Apple serial number, or Dell service tag. Enter any of these identifiers and the tool will auto-detect the type and decode the relevant information — manufacturer, model, manufacturing date, warranty status, and more.

Supported lookup types:
  • IMEI — 14 or 15-digit number found by dialing *#06# or in Settings > About Phone. Identifies manufacturer and model via TAC database.
  • Apple Serial — 12-character (pre-2021) serials decode factory, manufacture date, and model. Post-2021 10-character serials are randomized and cannot be decoded.
  • Dell Service Tag — 5-7 character alphanumeric code found on the bottom of Dell devices or via wmic bios get serialnumber. Returns model and warranty info.
